Design a complete, production-quality website and CMS/admin dashboard for **RuSoft Technologies**, a global software engineering company.

The design should feel like a premium international technology company, not a generic software agency or SaaS template.

## BRAND DIRECTION

Use the existing RuSoft Technologies logo as the primary brand identity.

Visual direction:

* Swiss International Style
* Editorial typography
* Modern brutalism, used carefully
* Digital architecture
* Minimal black and white visual system
* Strong grid
* Large typography
* Generous whitespace
* Thin geometric lines
* Subtle technical diagrams
* Restrained motion
* Premium and highly professional
* Avoid excessive gradients
* Avoid generic AI illustrations
* Avoid excessive rounded cards
* Avoid the typical "dark SaaS + purple gradient" aesthetic

Primary colors:

* Black: #080808
* White: #FFFFFF
* Off-white: #F5F5F3
* Gray: #707070
* Light border: #E5E5E5

Typography should be modern and highly readable, using a premium sans-serif such as Inter, Geist, Helvetica Neue, or a similar typeface.

The existing RuSoft geometric logo should influence the visual language of the website. Reuse its geometry subtly in backgrounds, transitions, diagrams, hover states, loading animations and decorative elements.
